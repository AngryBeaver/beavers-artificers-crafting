## 1.0.0
- the recipe "Tinker's Magic" (moved from Beaver's Crafting), now with its cost: one use of Tinker's Magic
- the Script "Tinker's Magic use": the feature's own uses, or a counter of this module (Intelligence modifier, back after a long rest)
- Replicate Magic Item: a recipe per plan, in folders by Artificer level (2, 6, 10, 14)
  - learn a plan by putting its recipe on the actor; removing it makes its items vanish
  - Scripts "Artificer level N" and "Replicate Magic Item" (items per long rest, each from a different plan)
  - more items than the level allows: the oldest vanishes; items that need attunement are attuned
  - +1 / +2 plans: the chosen base item gets the magic bonus
- every result is looked up when crafted: Dungeon Master's Guide / Player's Handbook first, then the SRD, then any
  compendium or world item with that name and type; none: an error says the item is not in the SRD 5.2
